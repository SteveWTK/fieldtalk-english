Mark plus wordmark — the signature for headers, splashes, covers and boards.

```jsx
<Lockup mark="open" size={32} />
<Lockup orientation="vertical" mark="crest" size={118} slogan="Da base ao palco global" />
<Lockup mark="crest" tone="monoInk" size={34} />
```

"GLOBAL PLAYER" is always Archivo 900 caps at 0.2em tracking and never translates. Only `slogan` localises.
